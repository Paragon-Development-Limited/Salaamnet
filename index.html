About SalaamNet

Wi-Fi for Everyone,
Everywhere in South Sudan

SalaamNet is a wholly-owned subsidiary of Paragon Development Limited, providing fixed and wireless internet services across the Republic of South Sudan. We currently operate 4G LTE mobile broadband in Juba, with active expansion plans for the rest of the country.

We are driven by a strong belief in South Sudan's future as an emerging economy and in the innovative, resilient spirit of its people.

Our Vision
Truly fast wireless internet of superior quality at affordable rates, available to the broadest spectrum of users across South Sudan.
Our Mission
A fully connected South Sudan, with digital access available to all segments of society at an affordable price with optimal quality and reliability.
